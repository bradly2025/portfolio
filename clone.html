Products

IWOMI offers a range of solutions ready for any type of businesses on multiple platforms

Softeller


Web and mobile application for instant money transfers from abroad to mobile money accounts in Cameroon (for a start); Communication credit

MOSA


MOSA is a POS (Point Of Sales) which includes a mobile commercial management application and integrates all means of payment in the ecosystem

ARES-REPORTING


Banking reporting solution integrating internal reporting, SPECTRA reporting, Risk Central, Payment incidents, Balance of payments, GACHA

BillerPro


A secure cash management solution, allowing companies to accept payments from their invoices online, and instantly credited to their bank accounts....

IWOMI PAY


IWOMI also has APIs to allow payment sites and applications to accept payments on their sites or e-commerce platforms.

IWOMI CORE


IWOMI CORE is an interface for the interconnection of Core Banking Systems with third-party systems (external to the Core Banking System)

Loan 360


Loan360 is a secure and scalable system that facilitates the credit processing and monitoring process. It allows customers to request, track, obtain and repay

IWAKAP


Web platform which enforces solidarity by offering its users the possibility of creating fundraising campaigns, and receiving support or funding from well-wishers around the world

BKWIKI


Mobile and web application that provides access to general information on banks in Africa, and also General Knowledge on the banking sector, its terminologies, functioning, structural organization and regulation
